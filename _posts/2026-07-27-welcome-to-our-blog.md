Welcome to our new blog! This is where we'll be sharing the stories, recipes, and small details behind every homemade Karachi Jumma Biryani order — from why we cook only on Fridays to what goes into our 6-day monthly dinner package.

## Why we started this blog

Homemade food is about more than just a meal — it's about the story behind it. We wanted a place to talk about our ingredients, our process, and the Karachi family recipes that inspired this whole idea, for anyone in Al Khobar looking for authentic, home-cooked Pakistani food.

## What to expect

- Recipe breakdowns for our most-requested dishes
- Tips on ordering ahead for Friday Biryani
- Updates on our monthly dinner package menu rotation
- Customer stories and photos

Stay tuned, and as always — if you're craving biryani, our WhatsApp is one tap away.
